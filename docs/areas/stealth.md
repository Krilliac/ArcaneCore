# Stealth and invisibility (1.12.1)

## Implemented in this checkout

- Stealth aura state sets `UNIT_FIELD_BYTES_1` creep and `PLAYER_FIELD_BYTES2` stealth bits. A new stealth application forces a visibility refresh through `NO_DETECT` then `STEALTH`; the last removal clears the bits and refreshes observers. `Map.UpdateVisibilityOf` emits an out-of-range update to viewers who lose the unit and a create update to viewers who gain it. Sources: `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Spells/SpellAuras.cpp:3631-3705`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:6437-6460`; the field layout and flag values are in `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Player.h:389-390` and the existing `UpdateFields` definitions.
- The existing periodic player detection pass applies the vmangos stealth skill and distance formula, rear arc penalty, line of sight, Hunter's Mark, group and GM exceptions. Sources: `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:6321-6461,6543-6616`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Player.cpp:22007-22052`. Stealth auras used by Rogue Stealth, Prowl and Shadowmeld enter this common path; there is no spell-ID special case in the visibility rule.
- Invisibility aura types 18 and 19 now use aura `MiscValue` as a type bit (0-31). Matching invisible types reveal one another; a detector reveals a type when its strongest detection aura of that type meets or exceeds the target's strongest invisibility level. Invisibility sets the player glow byte, hides the unit on application, refreshes on detect aura changes, and restores stealth state when invisibility fades or vice versa. A world boss creature detects invisibility. Sources: `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:6401-6439,6502-6541`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Spells/SpellAuras.cpp:3698-3705,3711-3779`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Player.h:390`. Aura numeric types are already defined in `SpellEnums.g.cs` from the vanilla spell aura reference.
- Creature proximity aggro now calls `ICreatureVisibility.CanCreatureSee` after hostility and range checks. It applies invisibility and the creature stealth formula; the `alert` result is exposed to a future creature reaction. Sources: `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Maps/GridNotifiersImpl.h:54-70`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:6401-6461,6543-6616`.
- Existing aura interrupt dispatch breaks stealth/invisibility on configured attack, cast, hostile damage, and self damage events. Source: `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Spells/Spell.cpp:3443-3456,3697-3714`, `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:3735-3751`. See [rogue aura interrupt integration](../integration/rogue-aura-interrupt.md).

## Limits and acceptance

- Vanish's one-second undetectable window, its root/snare/mark removal and recast behavior, and canceling its buff along with Rogue Stealth are not implemented. The existing Stealth aura gives its ordinary concealment only. `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp:6141` (`CanBeDetected`), `Player.h:1937,1957-1958` (`m_cannotBeDetectedTimer`) and `D:/refs/vmangos/src/game/Spells/SpellAuras.cpp:3675-3683` define the omitted behavior.
- Shapeshift form 30, mounted stealth removal, and the remaining interaction interrupt call sites depend on their owning systems; see [rogue](rogue.md) and [rogue aura interrupt integration](../integration/rogue-aura-interrupt.md). The `StealthServices` `alert` return does not yet drive the creature's reaction animation or distracted movement.
- Type 6 drunk-value detection is not connected to player intoxication state. Unrelated GM invisibility is handled separately. Runtime client acceptance and provider CI were not available in this worktree.

Real-client checks on build 5875: enter Rogue Stealth, Druid Prowl and Night Elf Shadowmeld near two players; verify each observer receives fade/reappear updates; test front/rear detection, Perception, Detect Invisibility, equal-type invisibility, and creature aggro. Repeat with a Vanish cast to confirm the documented gap before accepting retail fidelity.
