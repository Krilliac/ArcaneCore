## Deliberate behaviours and limits

- The hourly limit is checked on teleports only, not on the login re-entry, and is in memory (a restart clears it, as in vmangos). At the cap vmangos erases whichever expired entry its unordered map yields first; here the oldest. Whether retail 1.12 enforced the limit is unverified (both reference servers default it on).
- The respawn delay of the killed creature is `RespawnAtMs - CreatureMapSystem.ClockMs`, i.e. the delay the creature system drew at death. A creature without a spawn row counts as delay 0 (reset = now + 2 h).
- The saved-instance packets are sent when the player has arrived (after the worldport ack), not together with `SMSG_NEW_WORLD`, because the teleport service belongs to another lane. Real-client confirmation of the packets is still required.
- The raid bind flag is classic-db's `creature_template.ExtraFlags` bit 1 (`CreatureFlagExtraInstanceBind`); vmangos's own static flag `LOCK_TAPPERS_TO_RAID_ON_DEATH` is not set in that data.

## ScriptDev2 dungeon encounters (2026-10-08)

Maraudon (349) now has `instance_maraudon`, the larva/corruption spewers and Noxxion's timed casts. Sunken Temple (109) has the ordered Atal'alarion statues, the protector barrier and the Avatar of Hakkar ritual, waves, suppressor and flame handling. Blackrock Depths (230) extends the Tomb of the Seven with the Ring of Law, vault, Iron Hall, Flamelash runes, Grim Guzzler bar patrol and Plugger/Phalanx riot; scripted boss AIs include Flamelash, Dagran and Moira, General Angerforge and High Interrogator Gerstahn. The scripts use the instance creature death, evade, combat and reached-home hooks and successful game-object use. An object opened by the open-lock spell (Spell::EffectOpenLock → SendLoot → GameObject::Use) runs the same object scripts as CMSG_GAMEOBJ_USE, which matters because the classic-db objects are locked: the Atal'ai statues and Dark Iron ale mugs use lock 93 (spell 6477 Opening), the Thunderbrew kegs lock 99 (8386 Attacking), the relic coffer doors, Shadowforge braziers and eternal flames key locks 639, 799 and 520. The vault counts all twelve `go_relic_coffer_door` entries (174554-174564, 174566) and the Lyceum both braziers (174744, 174745). A trap opened with an Attacking lock is activated (mangos-classic SendLoot), which is how the Maraudon larva spewer is destroyed. Doom'rel's gossip (`boss_doomrel`) offers the Tomb of the Seven challenge; a dwarf it turns hostile takes its template faction back when it reaches home after a wipe (TEMPFACTION_RESTORE_REACH_HOME). Gossip scripts share the quest NPC slot through `QuestNpcServices.AddGossipScript`. Sources: mangos-classic `AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/kalimdor/maraudon/{instance_maraudon,boss_noxxion}.cpp`, `eastern_kingdoms/sunken_temple/{sunken_temple,sunken_templeScripts}.cpp`, and `eastern_kingdoms/blackrock_depths/{instance_blackrock_depths,blackrock_depths,boss_ambassador_flamelash,boss_emperor_dagran_thaurissan,boss_general_angerforge,boss_high_interrogator_gerstahn}.cpp` (named `SetData`, `OnCreatureDeath`, `OnCreatureEvade`, `Update`, event and AI methods).

ClassicDB z2815 has the Ring and Phalanx escort paths in `script_waypoint` and the negative speech IDs in `script_texts`; the importer now merges these into the existing movement and text tables. A content refresh is required for an already imported world database. No schema version was taken. ScriptDev2's other BRD quest escorts and gossip branches are outside this encounter slice: `at_shadowforge_bridge`, `npc_mistress_nagmara`, `npc_rocknot`, `npc_hurley_blackbreath`, the Windsor/Tobias/Dughal jail-break escorts and `npc_ironhand_guardian`; so are Grimstone's visibility toggles and The Challenge (9015) kill credit, and Sunken Temple's `at_shade_of_eranikus` (Malfurion). `spell_hakkar_summoned` (12948, despawn the evil circles) is not ported because nothing in classic-db z2815 casts 12948; the circles stay after the Avatar appears, as on mangos-classic with that database. The Ring of Law uses the area trigger's own centre (`SetArenaCenterCoords(pAt->x, pAt->y, pAt->z)`). The instance save string still lasts only until a server restart because the characters database lacks `instance.data` (see `docs/integration/eventai-instance-20261008.md`).
