## Deliberate behaviours and limits

- The hourly limit is checked on teleports only, not on the login re-entry, and is in memory (a restart clears it, as in vmangos). At the cap vmangos erases whichever expired entry its unordered map yields first; here the oldest. Whether retail 1.12 enforced the limit is unverified (both reference servers default it on).
- The respawn delay of the killed creature is `RespawnAtMs - CreatureMapSystem.ClockMs`, i.e. the delay the creature system drew at death. A creature without a spawn row counts as delay 0 (reset = now + 2 h).
- The saved-instance packets are sent when the player has arrived (after the worldport ack), not together with `SMSG_NEW_WORLD`, because the teleport service belongs to another lane. Real-client confirmation of the packets is still required.
- The raid bind flag is classic-db's `creature_template.ExtraFlags` bit 1 (`CreatureFlagExtraInstanceBind`); vmangos's own static flag `LOCK_TAPPERS_TO_RAID_ON_DEATH` is not set in that data.

## ScriptDev2 dungeon events (2026-10-08)

The Wave 4 `InstanceData`/`ScriptedInstance` state scripts are extended for Deadmines (36), Wailing Caverns (43), Shadowfang Keep (33), and Blackfathom Deeps (48). Deadmines has boss-death doors, scripted patrol GUIDs, the cannon sequence and Mr. Smite's spell/phase timing. Wailing Caverns has the Disciple's gossip, escort pauses and summoned ritual waves. Their quest-only Mysterious Chests respawn for a player with the completed, unrewarded quest. Shadowfang Keep has prisoner gossip and escorts, the Rethilgore speech, Vincent intro and staged defeat, Fenrus' Arugal summon/dialogue, Nandos' pack trigger, Arugal combat AI and voidwalker death count. Blackfathom has fire-gated shrine waves, the portal door and Fathom Stone/Baron Aquanis. Behaviours follow mangos-classic `src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/{eastern_kingdoms,kalimdor}/` per-dungeon functions cited in the script files.

`script_texts`, `script_waypoint` and `waypoint_path` rows in ClassicDB z2815 are now imported so the dungeon dialogue, the Shadowfang prisoner paths and the Disciple of Naralex escort can run. The disciple's gossip starts `waypoint_path` 3678 (79 points; the script stops at points 12, 30 and 70), as mangos-classic `GossipSelect_npc_disciple_of_naralex` does; the escort test walks those 79 points. The three `dbscripts_on_creature_movement` scripts on that path (367801-367803: the disciple's lines 1255, 1257 and 1263 with their talk/point emotes) are not run, as no creature-movement script is. Ragefire Chasm (389) has no ScriptDev2 script in the mangos-classic reference tree and has no new scripted instance class. The two script gossip option strings come from mangos-classic `sql/scriptdev2/scriptdev2.sql` because ClassicDB has no `script_gossip` table.

Remaining fidelity limits: Mr. Smite's three scripted weapon visuals use the exact ClassicDB z2815 item rows rather than a general creature-equipment content loader. The ScriptDev2 `UnarmedWoodcutter` and `ForsakenSkill` aura hooks are not wired because this server has no per-spell aura-script registry. Encounter strings persist across restarts since wave 7 (characters 42, `instance.data`). No real client run is recorded for these scripts.
## ScriptDev2 dungeon encounters (2026-10-08)

Maraudon (349) now has `instance_maraudon`, the larva/corruption spewers and Noxxion's timed casts. Sunken Temple (109) has the ordered Atal'alarion statues, the protector barrier and the Avatar of Hakkar ritual, waves, suppressor and flame handling. The scripts use the instance creature death, evade, combat and reached-home hooks and successful game-object use. An object opened by the open-lock spell (Spell::EffectOpenLock → SendLoot → GameObject::Use) runs the same object scripts as CMSG_GAMEOBJ_USE, which matters because the classic-db objects are locked: the Atal'ai statues and Dark Iron ale mugs use lock 93 (spell 6477 Opening), the Thunderbrew kegs lock 99 (8386 Attacking), the relic coffer doors, Shadowforge braziers and eternal flames key locks 639, 799 and 520. Doom'rel's gossip (`boss_doomrel`) offers the Tomb of the Seven challenge; a dwarf it turns hostile takes its template faction back when it reaches home after a wipe (TEMPFACTION_RESTORE_REACH_HOME). Gossip scripts share the quest NPC slot through `QuestNpcServices.AddGossipScript`.
