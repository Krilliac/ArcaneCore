# Loot conditions and chest gold

Lane `L9-loot-conditions-and-chest-gold` (branch `ccr-build/L9-loot-conditions-and-chest-gold`, merged after L4's roll
manager). Two small gaps of the game-object loot area closed: `condition_id` rows of the loot tables are now decided by the
conditions feature, and `gameobject_template.mingold/maxgold` are imported and paid by chests. Reference behaviour was read
from the mangos core at `/home/user/mangosserver/server` (evidence, not authority); ArcaneCore's design (world-thread loot,
immutable content, retained-write persistence) wins where they differ.

## Known gaps

* **Loot source not passed to conditions.** The condition is evaluated per viewer, as the reference's `AllowedForPlayer`
  does (closed by the loot-templates fidelity lane at the 2026-10-07 integration: `LootItem.AllowedLooters`), but without
  the loot source (`Conditions` is `Func<Player, uint, bool>`), so source-side condition types (area of the chest,
  creature-side checks) read "no source" and fail closed.
* **Durable instance chests pay nothing.** The chest record stored with the instance save (`loot_state`, characters
  schema, `LootStateDataModule`, outside this lane) has no money column and `LootBag.ToRecord` refuses a bag with gold, so
  `LootService.OpenDurableGameObject` generates no money even when the template names a range. Adding `gold` to the
  record and the award replay is a characters-schema step for the durable-loot owner.
* **Chest money is not split.** The reference splits every non-item loot's money among the looter's group members in
  range (`LootHandler.cpp:344-385`); ArcaneCore pays the taker whole, as before this lane. A chest's recipients include
  ungrouped late openers, so the corpse split (over recipients) cannot be reused as is; the split needs a group-membership
  check against the taker's current group.
* `.reload gameobject_template` (the live reload) rebuilds the content from the store and so picks the gold columns up;
  nothing lane-specific was added to it.
