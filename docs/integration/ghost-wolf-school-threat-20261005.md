# Ghost Wolf lifecycle and school-scoped spell threat

A cataloged Ghost Wolf shapeshift now owns form byte0x10 and its form holder, using pinned display4613 and scale0.80. The shared form visual refresh understands Ghost Wolf during transform and ModScale updates, restoring each visual owner and native state correctly. Removal of an older holder cannot clear a newer form. Mana/power, Druid Furor/boost mechanics and the independently produced movement-speed effect are not repaid or duplicated. Spell2645's40% speed effect is removed with its own holder, while unrelated speed auras remain.

Ghost Wolf removes Water Walk and auras marked AURA_INTERRUPT_SHAPESHIFTING_CANCELS through the existing holder-removal machinery, excluding the incoming holder. It requires a supplied shapeshift catalog row; the optional original SpellShapeshiftForm.dbc and the warrior-only fallback are unchanged. Tests use an explicitly synthetic catalog row. No original DBC values or unverified passive spell IDs were added. Shadowform and other unsupported form lifecycles remain separate work.

Primary form evidence: vmangos0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa SpellAuras.cpp Ghost Wolf display/scale/form apply/remove and generic interrupt behavior; mangos-classic8ec338a1704e7dcb1c0213eb7ed58f9231ade40f Ghost Wolf2645 imported ModShapeshift and ModIncreaseSpeed producer. Original client acceptance and original model/geometry assets remain pending.

Generated nonlethal direct and periodic spell damage now pass their owning SpellSystem's school-scoped ModThreat product to map combat exactly once. Effective healing retains the current0.5 baseline and hostile-reference distribution, then uses the same school modifier. Explicit SPELL_EFFECT_THREAT uses that calculator once as well. Matching school bits apply the product of (100+amount)/100; unrelated school auras do not participate.

No-harmful and no-new-reference suppression also reaches the zero-damage and AttackedBy boundary, preventing CreatureAI AttackStart from recreating a zero threat reference after suppression. The no-new flag can still raise an already-established reference. No-helpful suppression affects healing threat without erasing effective healing. Damage, durability, death, loot and normal white damage keep their existing paths; ordinary/white threat modifiers and lethal-threat ordering are not broadened here.

Primary threat evidence: pinned vmangos Unit::ApplyTotalThreatModifier, ThreatCalcHelper::CalcThreat, Unit spell damage and HostileRefManager::threatAssist. This slice adds no spell_threat table, flat threat metadata, SPELLMOD_THREAT talent handling, class-specific healing coefficient, or complete pet/totem threat eligibility. Those remain explicit limits rather than being inferred from absent data.

Focused tests cover form/display/scale restoration, stale holder ownership, concurrent transform and ModScale removal, Water Walk and generic shape cancellation, valid mana preservation, owned versus unrelated speed, school matching/product, direct and periodic damage, effective overheal, explicit single scaling, suppressed healing/damage and fresh-caster harmful/no-new/zero reference prevention. All source changes remain local on published repair50ecdb; complete qualification is reported separately. WoWWiki1.12.1 and Wowhead Classic remain version-checked supplemental information sources.
