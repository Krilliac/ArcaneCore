# Bot-think scan allocations (perf-tick lane, 2026-10-20)

Measured first, then changed. Live build 120ea4381, the managed-bot terrain benchmark at 400 bots and BenchmarkDotNet, all on one
machine (Ryzen 9 9900X3D) through the fleet's build slots, with other lanes building beside it: wall times are noisy, allocation
counts and GC counts are the firm numbers. Follows the first session's [quest-candidate cuts](perf-tick-20261019.md).

## What the measurements showed

- **Live world (<live-home> logs, 1,500 bots).** Tick body p50 44 ms, p99 74 ms; of it the features phase is about 18 ms at
  p50 (the bot feature nearly all of it, its think budget about 11 ms ending every tick) and the maps phase about 20 ms. The
  process allocates about 60 MB/s; gen0/gen1 collections land inside ticks (the slow-tick lines show 30 ms GC pauses).
- **Where a bot's think spends and allocates.** `ManagedPlayerbotTerrainBenchmarkTests` at 400 bots (warmup 40 s, hold 60 s) with
  `dotnet-trace` attached for 40 s of the hold (CPU samples and GC allocation ticks). Allocation by site (1,915 MiB sampled):
  `PlayerbotWorldDestinations.HasQuestCandidate` and `Update` (the LINQ distance ordering of the destination list, the set of
  ineligible givers, the enders/starters enumerators of `Eligible`, closures), `PlayerbotRisk.ChooseTarget` through
  `PlayerbotBrain.CollectTargets` (the death-loop check's closure and delegate per creature in sight, 124 MB) and
  `PlayerbotBrain.FindDefensiveAttacker` (OfType, Where, OrderBy and two ThenBy, about 140 MB): together about 513 MB.
  Their share of the world thread's CPU samples: HasQuestCandidate 10.0%, CollectTargets 7.3%, FindDefensiveAttacker 7.3%,
  Update 6.5%.

## Changes (behaviour unchanged)

| Path | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `PlayerbotHazards.CoversDeathLoop` | `_hazards.Any(lambda)`: a closure and a delegate per creature in sight | loop |
| `PlayerbotBrain.FirstDefensiveCandidate` | OfType, Where, OrderBy(unreachable), ThenBy(current target), ThenBy(distance), FirstOrDefault | one pass keeping the best by the same keys; of equals the first met wins, as the stable ordering did |
| `PlayerbotWorldDestinations.HasQuestCandidate` / `Update` | `OrderBy(distance)` (after three `Where` in `Update`) | `RankEntries`: the same filters in the same order, then the entries sorted by (distance, list position) into a reused array |
| the ineligible (entry, in home) set of both scans | a new `HashSet` per scan that met an ineligible giver | one set handed out with the ranking and cleared after |
| `Eligible` | `foreach` over the `IReadOnlyList` of enders and starters (an enumerator each) | indexed loops |
| `RefreshEntries` | its lambdas made a closure at every call, though the list is almost always kept | the LINQ moved to `OrderedDestinationSpawns`, run only on a rebuild |
| `VisibleTarget` | `VisibleObjects.Any(lambda)` | loop |

A ranking that runs inside another (none does today) takes a fresh array and set instead of overwriting the outer one.

## Results

`PlayerbotThinkAllocationTests` (new) assert each path answers as before and allocates nothing once warm. On the old code
(slot-logs `perf-tick-r2-red-4.log`) the GC-free windows allocated: CoversDeathLoop x512 45,056 bytes, FindDefensiveAttacker x64
51,712, HasQuestCandidate x64 41,984, HasQuestCandidate over an ineligible giver x64 16,384. All four pass now
(`perf-tick-r2-green-3.log`).

BenchmarkDotNet `--filter "*PlayerbotHazard*"` (new `PlayerbotHazardBenchmarks`, 32 creatures in sight):

| Benchmark | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| CoversDeathLoop x32 | 509.3 ns, 2.75 KB | 225.9 ns, 0 B |

`ManagedPlayerbotTerrainBenchmarkTests`, 400 bots, warmup 40 s, hold 60 s, the same world database copy, untraced runs:

| Run | bot feature alloc KiB/tick | gen0 / gen1 | GC pause ms | bot feature ms/tick | tick mean / p99 ms | thinks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before 1 | 2,095.7 | 68 / 16 | 568 | 9.73 | 13.66 / 33.2 | 473,140 |
| before 2 | 2,064.0 | 62 / 11 | 515 | 10.20 | 14.71 / 45.2 | 461,064 |
| after 1 | 1,310.0 | 43 / 7 | 422 | 8.25 | 11.91 / 31.6 | 476,455 |
| after 2 | 1,326.8 | 43 / 8 | 421 | 8.24 | 11.91 / 30.9 | 477,610 |

The bot feature allocates about 37% less per tick and the hold sees about a third fewer gen0 collections, for the same number of
thinks (at 400 bots the thinks are bound by the think interval, not by the time budget). Traced again after the change (1,349 MiB
sampled), none of the rewritten sites is among the 60 largest allocation sites (the smallest listed is 4.8 MB), and their share of
the world thread's CPU samples fell to HasQuestCandidate 1.4%, CollectTargets 1.6%, Update 0.5%, FindDefensiveAttacker below 0.4%.

## Limits

- The terrain benchmark's own think recorder (`ThinkStats.Add`, `PlayerbotThinkProbe.Phases`) is about a sixth of its world-thread
  samples and a fifth of its allocation; that is test harness, not server code, and is the same before and after.
- Not changed: `PlayerbotCombatView.Build` and `Describe` (now the largest server-side think cost, about 9% of samples),
  `PlayerbotEquipment.Carried` (LINQ orderings of the bags), `SpellSystem.GetActiveCooldowns`, `QuestNpcServices.HasAreaTrigger`
  (a closure per call), and the live per-tick work outside the thinks (motion pump and party intake for every bot).
- The live bot think budget ends every tick, so on the live server the gain shows as more thinks per tick and fewer collections,
  not as a shorter features phase; the next deploy's cadence and GC counter lines are the check.
