## Next boss-data boundary: Scarlet Monastery

ClassicDB z2815 `creature_template` gives entries 4542 (Fairbanks), 3974 (Loksey) and 3983 (Vishas) `AIName='EventAI'` with no ScriptName. Its `creature_ai_scripts` rows already cast Fairbanks' Curse/Dispel/Fear/Sleep/Heal/Shield and handle his 28441 relay, cast Loksey's Bloodlust and Battle Shout, and cast Vishas' Shadow Word: Pain with pull/health/kill speech. The vmangos C++ differs: `boss_high_inquisitor_fairbanks.cpp` heals at 25% after a 30 s timer (ClassicDB EventAI triggers Heal at 30%), `boss_houndmaster_loksey.cpp` summons a Scarlet Hound (17164) at pull (the EventAI rows contain no summon), and `boss_interrogator_vishas.cpp` casts 2767 while ClassicDB EventAI casts 14032 with a different interval. Registering new C# AIs would replace these imported EventAI rows, including Fairbanks' Ashbringer relay, so this lane leaves the three bosses on their current data scripts until one complete Scarlet conversion can preserve that event. These are concrete vmangos differences, not an assertion that the bosses have no combat scripting.

Ironaya has the same compatibility issue: ClassicDB z2815 `creature_ai_scripts` rows 722801-722803 cast 8374, 10101 and 11876, while vmangos `EK/uldaman/boss_ironaya.cpp` gives Knock Away and War Stomp one-time health thresholds. The imported altar/door scripts also start her movement. A vmangos-style explicit AI must be integrated with that release route and must not silently remove the imported behavior.

## Independent review follow-up on `ea06e108` (uncommitted)

The lever's vmangos check was inverted in the first commit: `GO_STATE_READY` is 1, so its normal use and ClassicDB on-use relay must run while the Ironclad door is closed. The cannon instead opens the door to `GO_STATE_ACTIVE_ALTERNATIVE` (2); the shared door helper now accepts that state, including when a completed door is created later. A second cannon click after the button resets leaves the door open and does not restart the alarm. `CreatureMapSystem.RandomInt(1500, 4000)` is inclusive at both ends (`NextInt64(min, max + 1)`), matching `urand`.

The Shadowfang reveal is deliberately marker-specific: ClassicDB's 7200/7200 wolf guard stays visible, while a vmangos 7201/7202 guard is hidden until its boss dies. A boss with no matching hidden guard no longer arms a timer. Late-grid retries are limited to five checks at one-second intervals. Blackfathom's completion poll now treats a despawned wave creature as finished; the Deadmines gunpowder summon uses vmangos' timer that keeps counting down on a corpse; the overseer continues its route when its first spline ends within three yards of the point. Ectoplasm's reset invokes `RemoveAllAuras`, and idle ticks no longer remove eight auras repeatedly.

Nine built regression cases failed against `ea06e108` for the reported behaviors (lever, two cannon state checks, saved-door state, unmarked wolf guard, despawned BFD wave, dead summon timer, short overseer arrival, idle Ectoplasm). After the fixes, those nine passed.
