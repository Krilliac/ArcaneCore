The placement buffer (90 yd plus spawn wander radius) and maximum local level gap (3) are ArcaneCore safety choices. The hostile-spawn check uses the same build-5875 FactionTemplate fields as `FactionTemplateDbcReader` and considers only a known hostile template; player reputation, event-gated spawns and patrol paths can change live outcomes. If no same-zone linked graveyard passes, population reports `placement-unsafe` instead of teleporting that bot. This can leave the target population underfilled. Existing bots already in these clusters are protected by route/recovery hazards, but the placement change does not relocate them merely because their original graveyard is unsafe.

Gear is an observed amplifier, with timing unknown: the later character snapshot shows 8-10 equipped items at zero durability for Geora, Tonglaern, Viasthaend, Shoutrekrir, Trigoun, Zufliath, Eleiariahi, Shaekrosli, Veisluk and Vezebrek. Their equipment slots are populated and they have money; `PlayerbotTownGoals.TryRepair` already repairs at a visible repair NPC, but the issues do not record when those items broke or whether a repair NPC was reachable before each death. The failures cannot be attributed to missing gear at the first encounter from this snapshot. Live-client death-loop cessation and repair of already damaged bots remain unverified.
