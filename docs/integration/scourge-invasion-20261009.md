# Scourge invasion state — 2026-10-09

The six `CONDITION_WORLD_SCRIPT` invasion fields in ClassicDB z2815 are 2259-2264. `mangos-classic` `WorldState::IsConditionFulfilled` answers each with `GetSIRemaining(zone) > 0`, regardless of the condition's second value. Its six zone definitions start with two Necropolises in Azshara, Blasted Lands, Burning Steppes and Eastern Plaguelands, and three in Tanaris and Winterspring. The copied z2815 world has exactly 14 `Necropolis health` (16421) spawns, each assigned to its matching event 90-95.

Characters schema 45 stores the invasion's enabled state, battles won, last defeated zone, remaining count and next attack time per zone, and an idempotent death row per static Necropolis Health spawn. Starting server-side event 17 initializes the six counts; the world feature runs each zone event while its remaining count is positive. Necropolis Health's AI responds to the reference's camp-death communique (28351) with a triggered zap (28386), and dies after three zaps. Its death decrements the saved zone count once; when that count reaches zero, the condition becomes false, the zone event stops, and a 45-60 minute reattack is scheduled. Once at most one zone remains active, a due different zone can restart. The saved battle count selects milestone events 96/97 and, at 150 victories, 98/99. Stopping event 17 clears the invasion state.

An imported-world loopback test enters Azshara, delivers three zap hits to the actual Necropolis Health AI, and observes one decrement of the live condition count.
