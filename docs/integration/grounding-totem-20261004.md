# Grounding Totem spell redirection — 2026-10-04

`SPELL_AURA_SPELL_MAGNET` (96) is registered by a discovered spell module. A hostile explicit enemy spell now selects the magnet aura's original caster, spends its protection charge before hit resolution, and sends the redirected hit/miss GUID and explicit target in `SMSG_SPELL_GO`. All later explicit effects of that cast use the redirected unit; redirected chains terminate there.

The implementation uses the exact caster lifetime token already used by periodic and area auras. A dead, removed, revoked or replaced caster cannot receive redirected spells merely because a GUID matches. Selection runs when the spell finishes its cast bar, so protection removed during preparation cannot absorb it. Each cast copies its target request before selection, allowing callers to reuse their original request safely. Quest settlement holds exclude magnets whose state cannot currently change.

The retail eligibility rules follow vmangos commit `0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa`:

- [SpellCaster.cpp:31](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa/src/game/Objects/SpellCaster.cpp#L31): magic damage class, or special spell visual 7250; excludes poison dispel, abilities, `NO_REDIRECTION` and `SUPPRESS_TARGET_PROCS`. The magnet must be alive and on the caster's map.
- [Spell.cpp:2227](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa/src/game/Spells/Spell.cpp#L2227): only the explicit enemy selector consults magnet auras, and a redirected chain stops at the magnet. Area and ordinary friendly/caster selectors do not redirect.
- [Spell.cpp:249](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa/src/game/Spells/Spell.cpp#L249): Arcane Missiles' mage family / channel bit 11 has a special `UNIT_CASTER / NONE` target shape. Its channel aura is placed on the selected enemy. This source exception does not rewrite the explicit target block.
- [Spell.cpp:7579](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa/src/game/Spells/Spell.cpp#L7579): Grounding's aura 8179 bypasses incoming creature type restrictions. Generic magnets still respect creature type and player-only restrictions; the Arcane Missiles caster selector skips those restrictions, as the source does.
- [SpellAuras.h:210](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa/src/game/Spells/SpellAuras.h#L210): zero charges means unlimited; a miss still spends a finite charge. The charge budget applies once to a selected cast, including its multiple effects.

The existing cast pipeline checks the original victim's range and line of sight. The pinned magnet selector adds neither a range check nor a line-of-sight check against the magnet. This behavior is preserved without a new configuration switch. Spawned and unselectable magnets, unsupported creature type masks and player-only spells fall back to the original victim.

Consuming the final charge removes the caster's matching source aura and its existing party area children. Redirected damage follows normal combat and can kill and unsummon the totem. A miss or a non-damaging spell consumes protection while leaving the totem alive until its ordinary lifetime ends. Removing an aura does not create an artificial totem death. Redirected channels stop when the magnet dies or leaves, and cancelling the channel removes the aura on the actual magnet target, including Arcane Missiles' special selector.

## Verification

`SpellMagnetTests` exercise real casts and aura application: all effects redirected together; reusable request isolation; mixed `UNIT_ENEMY / UNIT` and `UNIT_ENEMY / NONE` effects; finite and unlimited charges; misses; chain termination; all eligibility exclusions; party child/source removal; dead and revoked casters; GM/spawning/unselectable restrictions; cast completion timing; special visual; original victim LOS failure; absence of an extra magnet range/LOS gate; Arcane Missiles' selector exception and cancellation/death cleanup.

`GroundingTotemWorldTests` exercise the production World host with a real socket client, creature summon, ownership fields, party area propagation, magic spell application, redirected spell-go packet and normal damage/death cleanup. The fixture retains Grounding aura ID 8179 to prove the creature type exemption while using synthetic summon/passive/hostile spell IDs. Its damage and non-damage cases also prove protection is consumed without later regeneration. The non-damage hostile aura case reaches the totem's intrinsic aura immunity, produces an `Immune2` spell-go miss for the redirected GUID and leaves the totem alive; its next cast reaches the unprotected owner.

Build/test results are recorded in the tranche's combined continuation notes. Proprietary client/world content fixtures and a real build-5875 client remain separate acceptance checks. This does not introduce a missile travel-time engine or expand the server's existing general visibility, creature-target, charm or area-aura restrictions beyond the magnet path. The magnet eligibility subset covers GM mode but does not yet implement GM hidden visibility, an existing charmer, computed charm-level limits or the `NOT_ON_AOE_IMMUNE` creature restriction from the full source `CheckTarget`. An ineligible low-rank charm can therefore consume Grounding protection before the separate totem immunity rule rejects its aura; that source discrepancy remains a bounded follow-up.
