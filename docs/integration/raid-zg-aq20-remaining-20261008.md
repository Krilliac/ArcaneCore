## Intake review (2026-10-08)

The coordinator's intake fixed these defects in the Codex diff:

- **Thekal:** a zealot rechecked its resurrection every tick, so both zealots stood back up as soon as Thekal left SPECIAL for the tiger phase. The timer is now one-shot as in `ACTION_RESSURECTION`, and Thekal cancels both zealots' timers (`CanPreventAddsResurrect`/`PreventRevive`). Thekal's death now despawns the zealots with their emote, his evade now evades them (a fake-dead zealot otherwise stayed down forever after a wipe), he rises after one second when both zealots are already down, and he is uninteractible without melee during the five-second tiger transition.

- **Ossirian:** only one crystal ever stood at a time; the reference raises five more ten seconds into the fight and one per weakness.
- **Moam/Buru:** missing emotes, Moam's reset (mana and fiends), and Buru's 1,000,000-threat random-player target (also on aggro).

## Rework after the second review (2026-10-08)

Each reviewer issue was fixed; none was disproved.

1. **Mandokir (material).** `MandokirAI.OnMovementInform(POINT_DOWNSTAIRS)` removes `UNIT_FLAG_IMMUNE_TO_PLAYER` and calls `SetInCombatWithZone`, so the downstairs run after the Vilebranch Speaker's SPECIAL ends in the engage. Ported as well: the Threatening Gaze AuraScript (`ThreateningGazeAuraModule` raises AI events 1000/1001 when 24314 goes on or off; the AI notes the watched player's threat and charges with 24315, after summoning with 25104 when out of line of sight, if that threat rose), the charge threat wipe, the every-third-kill Level Up (24312) with SAY_DING_KILL and Jin'do's SAY_GRATS_JINDO, the chained-spirit revive (24341) of players he or Ohgan kills, the unmount (remounted at home), Ohgan joining his victim and EMOTE_RAGE.
2. **Rajaxx (material).** `RajaxxAI` is gone and `RaidBossAI.Create` has no Rajaxx entry, so his ClassicDB EventAI runs him unchanged (range-gated Disarm and Summon Player, the threat drop with yell 11067, the death yell, Double Attack on spawn). `RuinsOfAhnQirajInstance.OnCreatureDeath` sets slot 1 DONE when he dies. `Rajaxx_HisOwnPullDoesNotStartTheArmy_AndACaptainEvadeFailsIt` now completes the event through the instance hook.
3. **Thekal fake death.** `OnLethalDamage` and the fake-death tick no longer call `CombatStop`; they stop the swing only (`AttackStop`), ignore `AttackedBy`, and leave combat and threat in place. Rising calls victim selection again, so a wipe during the fake death ends in an evade at the rise and FAIL at home. Because there is no combat stop inside the `DamageTaken` callback, the later combat re-link of the same hit is harmless.
4. **Andorov after Rajaxx.** On DONE the instance sends Andorov AI event 1000 (AI_EVENT_CUSTOM_A): gossip and vendor flags, a trade line in his gossip, and 135 seconds later SAY_ANDOROV_DESPAWN and `ForcedDespawn(2500)`. The gossip start removes IMMUNE_TO_NPC from Andorov and every Kaldorei Elite.
5. **Minor fidelity.** Each wave is sent with `SetInCombatWithZone` plus `AttackClosestEnemy`. Wave membership is fixed when the event starts, and each soldier within 50 yards of a captain belongs to the closest captain only, so a wave no longer pulls a neighbour's soldiers. Ossirian's Supreme recast checks that the aura is absent and yells SAY_SUPREME_1-3. The crystal has no encounter-state gate. Andorov's dialogue is split as in the reference: INTRO_1-2 at the gossip choice and INTRO_3, INTRO_4 and ATTACK_START on arrival at the attack point.
6. **Ossirian crystal performance.** `EnsureCrystal` no longer runs every tick. `RespawnFirstCrystal` sets a flag on events (instance start, a 15590 trigger created, Ossirian's reset, a failed attempt), and `Update` reads only that flag until the map has its game objects and triggers. The "keep one crystal standing" fallback is gone.
7. **Tests.** The new tests cover the gaps. Mandokir's downstairs engage runs over world ticks through the real point movement. Thekal's fake death runs over world ticks, from the lethal hit through the wipe to FAIL. Rajaxx's EventAI is checked for range-gated Disarm at melee range. The test does not assert that Summon Player is absent, because target type 9 never resolves (see Remaining limits) and the assertion would prove nothing. The other new tests cover wave membership, Andorov's full event and vendor despawn, the gaze, the kills and revive, the mount, the pre-pull crystal with no tick replacement, and Supreme's aura check.

## Remaining limits

- **Rajaxx EventAI target types (host gap, outside this lane):** ClassicDB 1534104 casts Summon Player at target type 9 (`TARGET_T_HOSTILE_RANDOM_NOT_TOP_PLAYER`). `EventAiContext.SelectTarget` resolves types 0-7 and 10-12 and 15, but not 8 or 9, so the host's EventAI fails that action. Disarm, Thundercrash, the threat drop, Frenzy and the yells resolve. This finding comes from reading the code, not from a test.
- **Threatening Gaze wiring:** `ThreateningGazeAuraModule` forwards 24314 holder add/remove to the caster's AI. The tests drive the AI events directly, and no test applies the real aura from a creature caster.
- **Mandokir:** vmangos' level increase per kill (`SetLevel`) and its Vilebranch-dead reset placement (`CheckVilebranchState`) are not ported; MC's behaviour is. Spirit despawn is direct instead of through 24342's script effect.
- **Edge event day:** VM `instance_zulgurub.cpp::SetData(TYPE_RANDOM_BOSS)` maps active events 29-32 to the four bosses, but `SpawnRandomBoss` is disabled there. ArcaneCore has the event service and ClassicDB event rows, but this instance script has no injected active-event state or Edge altar summon entry point. The four combat AIs can run when spawned; automatic event-day selection is still absent.
- **Rajaxx waves:** the host does not import the reference's `AQ20_*` creature-group string IDs, so waves are the closest-captain partition above. A wave follows as soon as the previous one is dead; MC shortcuts only the last captain's wave (`OnCreatureGroupDespawn(AQ20_ZERRAN)`) but documents the "after the previous wave is finished" rule. Andorov's evade returns him to the intro or attack point through his home position instead of a scripted MovePoint, and the elites follow him again from their home after a fight. Neither path has a world-tick test. His vendor list depends on the imported `npc_vendor` rows for 15471, which were not checked.
- **Gahz'ranka:** fishing/lure summon is outside the instance script hooks used here. His combat AI is present for a spawned creature; a real lure-to-spawn path has not been verified.
- **Ayamiss:** the host cannot run the reference's database movement path for her landing. She moves toward home; larva fixate/feed and swarm formation need a live-content test.
- **Spell and visuals:** summoned adds, charm and aura behavior rely on imported Spell data and the existing spell host. Buru's egg applies the reference damage through `MapCombat` at death because the host excludes dead casters from `SpellHitTarget`; the triggered explosion/hatchling spells remain for visuals and summoning. Original client visuals, timing under casts and a multiplayer raid clear are unverified.
- **Jin'do:** the boss casts Brain Wash Totem, Powerful Healing Ward, Hex, Delusions, Shade and Banish, but the summoned shade (invisible, hittable only under Delusions, Shadow Shock) and the Brain Wash totem's mind control have no AIs yet.
- **Ossirian:** the Sand Vortex summons, the storm weather, the ten-second run-speed increase and the random intro lines are not ported. The weakness is cast at Ossirian, so a crystal needs a living Ossirian.
- ZG's retained ScriptDev2 eight-slot save format has no persisted slots for Jin'do, Gahz'ranka or Edge of Madness. Kurinnaxx/Hakkar wave-7 tests still cover their existing logic. No real-client or hosted-CI validation is claimed.
