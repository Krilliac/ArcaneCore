The Bug Trio wipe can restore a dead creature before its four-second corpse despawn fires. `CreatureMapSystem.ForceRespawn` now clears that pending despawn for the creature it restores; the wipe test advances the world clock past the old deadline.

The Bug Trio uses ScriptDev2's specific Kri cleave/cloud, Yauj fear/ravage, and Vem knock away IDs instead of vmangos' documented temporary substitutions. Yauj brood points use vmangos' 40-yard and room-center line-of-sight checks. The bounded retry skips a brood if no valid point exists; a live map with the vmap/terrain set is still needed to assess those points. The test creature data are synthetic; no content row was invented in production.

Huhuran (15509) now has a registered encounter AI. Its timers and 30-percent/five-minute Berserk follow vmangos `boss_huhuran.cpp`; the instance's Huhuran slot reaches InProgress, Fail and Done through a normal pull, wipe and kill. `HuhuranSpellModule` makes Wyvern Sting 26180 choose the closest ten targets and Poison Bolt Volley 26052 the closest fifteen, as the vmangos target-map scripts do. ## 2026-10-09 Viscidus combat hook

The creature AI host now forwards landed white melee hits before their damage through `CreatureAI.OnMeleeHitReceived`; misses, evades, dodges, parries and resists are excluded. A focused combat test verifies that ordering and filtering. Viscidus needs this in addition to the existing spell-hit hook: vmangos `boss_viscidus.cpp` counts frost spell hits toward slow/freeze at 100/150/200, while `UnitAuraProcHandler.cpp` counts physical hits on the frozen aura toward crack/shatter/explosion at 50/100/150. The hit hook is ready; Viscidus's phases, globs, toxin clouds and death prevention are still unimplemented.

The imported Viscidus glob spells 25865-25884 use `SUMMON_WILD` with `TARGET_LOCATION_DATABASE` and twenty distinct map-531 positions. `SummonService.EffectSummonWild` now resolves that destination from `SpellStore` for the summon effect, including a same-map check, before placing the first creature. The imported-world runtime test casts 25865 from the loaded Viscidus spawn and confirms a glob appears at its database position. That test failed with a caster-near spawn before the fix and passed afterward. This verifies the spawn path; Viscidus phase logic remains unimplemented.
