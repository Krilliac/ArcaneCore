## Deliberate acceptance limits

Item containers remain refused through ordinary `CMSG_OPEN_ITEM` until their consumed/remaining contents can be persisted atomically with awarded inventory. Saving the container and its awards in an ordinary character snapshot cannot reconstruct which drops were consumed after relog. The refusal leaves an existing loot window intact and does not consume or charge the item.

For chest loot, retained partial contents cover grid unload/reload within a running world. They do not establish durable consumed state across process restart, instance unload/reload or a reused spawn lifetime. **Instanced chest rewards must remain refused pending a durable per-instance consumed-state owner**; root must confirm this cross-feature gate in the integrated source and tests before marking it included. Partial grid retention must not be advertised as permanent chest persistence.

Quest reward effects which create permanent items, learn spells or otherwise require durable collaborators remain refused when those effects cannot be included in the atomic reward transaction. Handler registration or a successful transient cast is insufficient. The correction preserves the accepted quest and available reward when declining such a turn-in; it does not grant first and attempt a best-effort rollback later. This is an acceptance boundary, not delivery of all reward categories.
