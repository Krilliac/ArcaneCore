# Explicit helpful targeting and next-swing spell packets

The cast check now validates positive spells whose explicit primary selector is UnitFriend or UnitFriendChainHeal. Known hostile faction-template reactions in either direction return BadTargets. Friendly, neutral, missing-template and no-catalog NPC targets retain their existing liveness/range/LOS checks and remain eligible. Player-player team and started-duel policy stays in the existing combat hook. CantTargetSelf remains enforced. Harmful spells and generic Unit/TargetB selectors are not newly classified by this helper.

The neutral boundary follows pinned vmangos0e3ff01e76d4758e8a7c3108b2717cc785ed56fa, Object.cpp3818-3856 and Spell.cpp5588-5602: the helpful relation threshold is Unfriendly, with hostility tested in both directions. CMaNGOSclassic8ec338a1704e7dcb1c0213eb7ed58f9231ade40f uses a stricter Friendly threshold for CanAssist; it is recorded as a difference rather than applied silently. Complete reputation, forced ranks, GM/FFA, PvP assistance and affecting-player rules are outside this bounded slice. Existing SpellInfo positivity remains a simplified source model, including its documented trigger-spell limits.

A queued next-swing spell now owns one SMSG_ATTACKERSTATEUPDATE instead of relying on the skipped white hit. It carries the queued spell ID and original5875 NO_ACTION bit0x00010000, school masks, actual resolved damage/absorb/resist/crit values and available victim-state outcomes. The white-swing builder retains spell ID zero. Eligibility and cast/target context are captured before effects or lethal cleanup can clear Combat.Victim.

Direct damage components are collected through the same ApplyEffects outcome, including effects after a victim pointer change. Their totals and crit state are combined, same-school sub-damage rows coalesce, and the attacker-state packet precedes all deferred non-melee damage logs for that outcome. Zero, dodge and parry paths emit no invented damage. Packet deduplication applies to the same live cast/target; it does not add a durable journal or execute damage, procs, rage or item costs twice.

Packet provenance: pinned vmangos Spell.cpp1289-1290,1425-1429,4947-4988 and wow_messages70abb9deff0bb63440d8aeb4386b820653e8a176 original1.12 attacker-state layout. Current spell outcomes do not retain a full meleeHitInfo or blocked damage amount; this change publishes available resolved fields and does not claim complete block/physics parity. Original client/DBC acceptance remains pending. WoWWiki1.12.1 and Wowhead Classic remain version-checked supplemental sources.

Focused verification covers positive/negative selector distinction, known hostility in either direction, neutral/unknown/no-catalog acceptance, self and duel behavior, exact queued/white packet identity, school masks, zero/dodge/parry/lethal packets, same-school multi-effect totals and packet-before-log ordering after victim cleanup. The duel cleanup fixture now applies the positive opponent buff before the duel starts, preserving its original cleanup assertions while using a legal source scenario. Complete qualification results are recorded separately; all new changes remain local on published repair50ecdb.
