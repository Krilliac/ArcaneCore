Starter FAQ for the GM ticket assistant. One topic per paragraph. The assistant answers only from this text; edit it, or point
World:GmTicketAssistant:FaqPath at your own file. Keep every answer short and true for this server.

Rules: Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or threats. No cheating, exploits, botting for profit, or selling accounts. No
advertising other servers. Staff decisions are final in game; if you disagree, file a ticket and a GM will read it.

Staff: Game masters answer tickets in the order they arrive. An assistant answers simple questions and fixes a few clear problems
(stuck, cannot resurrect) when the server can verify them. Anything else is left for a person. The assistant never gives items,
gold, experience or levels, and never bans, mutes or kicks anyone.

Bots: Some characters on this server are server-controlled bots. They fill groups and the world, can be asked to join a party, and
answer short questions in chat. A bot may be told to stop talking to you by whispering it "ai off". Bots follow the same rules as
players and cannot be traded for real money.

Stuck: If your character is stuck, type .unstuck. A living character gets a buff and dies when it ends; a ghost is sent to the nearest
graveyard. It needs level 10, cannot be used in combat, on a battleground, or while on a flight path, and has a cooldown. If that does
not help, file a ticket from the help window and say where you are.

Dead: If you cannot resurrect, release your spirit and run to your corpse, or talk to a spirit healer at a graveyard. Resurrecting at
a spirit healer gives resurrection sickness for a while and damages your gear. If you are a ghost and cannot reach your corpse, file a
ticket and say so.

Tickets: Open the help window (the question mark button) to file a ticket. One open ticket per character. You can edit or withdraw it
there. Answers are shown to you in the chat window when you are online.

Quests: If a quest does not progress or cannot be handed in, abandon it and take it again from the quest giver once. If it is still
broken, file a ticket with the quest name. A GM will look at it.

Lost items: Check your bags, bank and mailbox first. Items are not restored by the assistant; a GM reviews each report.

Commands: .unstuck frees a stuck character (see Stuck). Chat commands begin with a dot; commands for staff are not available to players.
